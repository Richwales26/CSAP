# CSAP Copy Guide (plain words)

Use short, calm, clear words.

- Protected → "Your car is locked and watched."
- Last-known → "Last seen 12 min ago. Not live."
- Disconnected → "No link. Here is what still works."
- Unknown → "We do not know yet. Check again soon."
- SOS → "We will tell your contacts and help you call for help. CSAP is not an emergency service."
- Failed action → "Not done. Try again. Your car is unchanged."
- Not sure → "Sent, but not confirmed yet."
- Theft worry → "Possible issue. Check your car. We raised watch level."
- No panic words. No promise like "safe" or "police called" unless true.
